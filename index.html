I'm a software engineer with 9+ years of experience designing and building backend and full-stack systems. Since 2022 I've been an SDE II at Amazon in Cairo, working on digital payments.

Before Amazon, I was a Principal Software Engineer at Softec Technologies and spent three years as a Senior Software Developer at Luftborn, shipping web applications with .NET, C#, and modern JavaScript frameworks. I started out as a freelance full-stack developer on Upwork.

I enjoy deep dives into hard problems, turning ambiguous requirements into clean architecture, and helping other engineers grow. These days I'm especially excited about AI and machine learning, and how they can make everyday work better.
